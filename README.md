# Nusa Drive — Kota Nusa

[![make.fun](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-01.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01)
[![rank](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-01/radeisv/nusa-drive-r5/rank.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01)
[![reward](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-01/radeisv/nusa-drive-r5/reward.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01)

An open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6, built from scratch with
three.js. Explore the island city of **Kota Nusa** on foot and by car, steal
vehicles, outrun the **Polisi Nusa** (wanted 0–5 stars, helicopter with
spotlight at 3+), and run four story missions — from meeting Sinta at the pier
to the big harbour heist — with combat, crew help, a full HUD with a circular
minimap, synthesized WebAudio sound and music, and save/load.

Play it: serve `game/` with any static server and open `game/index.html`
in desktop Chrome. No network requests, no build step.

- `WASD` drive / walk · `E` enter & steal car · `SPACE` brake
- `MOUSE` aim · `CLICK` shoot · `1–4` weapons · `Z/X/C` crew help
- `M` next mission · `ESC` pause

Entry for the [make.fun GTA 6 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01).
All names, characters, art, story and music are original.
